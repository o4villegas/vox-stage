STYLE REFERENCE · DIRECTION A · "INSTRUMENT" · 2026-09-08

A precision instrument on a dark stage. The only warmth is your voice.

Cool, exact, data-forward: graphite-black surfaces, a calibrated tick grid, white-hot type and mono readouts. Every human signal - the note you are singing - is rendered as living light whose temperature is the feedback: cold when flat, white-hot when on pitch, hot when sharp. Readable from a mic stand, familiar in the hand (iPhone conventions), and quiet everywhere except two moments: the range reveal and the end-of-song result.

Principle 1
Numbers you can trust

Cents, note names and keys are always visible in a mono readout. Nothing is rounded into a smiley face.

Principle 2
Light is feedback

The voice glow is not decoration: its temperature encodes pitch error, so you can read it from a metre away.

Principle 3
Quiet, then cinematic twice

Motion is 120-320 ms and purposeful. The range reveal and the result are the only full-screen moments.
