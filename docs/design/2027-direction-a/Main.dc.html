STYLE REFERENCE · DIRECTION A "INSTRUMENT" · REVISION 2 · MORE DEPTH, NEW TYPE

A precision instrument on a dark stage. The only warmth is your voice.

Cool, exact, data-forward: layered graphite surfaces with lit edges, a stage floor that recedes into the dark, white-hot type and mono readouts. Every human signal, the note you are singing, is living light whose temperature is the feedback: cold when flat, white-hot when on pitch, hot when sharp. Readable from a mic stand, familiar in the hand, and quiet everywhere except two moments: the range reveal and the end-of-song result.

Principle 1
Numbers you can trust

Cents, note names and keys are always visible in a mono readout. Nothing is rounded into a smiley face.

Principle 2
Light is feedback, depth is place

The voice glow encodes pitch error; elevation encodes what is nearest to you: now, this song, this action.

Principle 3
Quiet, then cinematic twice

Motion is 120–320 ms and purposeful. The range reveal and the result are the only full-screen moments.

Light from above
One soft cone from the top of the screen. Never coloured; never on top of text.
Lit edge, real shadow
Every raised object has a highlight on its top edge and a shadow that falls down and away. Nothing is flat, nothing floats without a shadow.
